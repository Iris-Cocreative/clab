The science of connection

Disconnection is the root cause.
Connection is the remedy.

The Watts Connectedness Study confirms the more disconnected we feel, the more we suffer. The more connected we feel to ourselves, others and the living world, the more we thrive. See the science

Now, FMRI scans let us see what is happening in the brain.

The modern world disconnects us not only from ourselves, but from each other and the living world.

This disconnection limits our human potential. It is the source of our discontent, and the wider suffering we see in the world around us.

Conventional therapy, coaching and spirituality tend to focus on the individual. This can be helpful but is incomplete.

C.Lab offers a more complete path to a fulfilling life and loving world. We call it the 5 Movements.
